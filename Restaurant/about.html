About us

Bringing the taste of Kerala to Abu Dhabi.

Traditional inspiration, familiar flavours and a welcoming dining experience in Musaffah M45.

Our philosophy

ഓർമയിലേക്കൊരു മടക്ക യാത്ര

A Journey Back to Memories.

New Indian Bakery & Restaurant aims to deliver Kerala's traditional foods with their original, familiar character. Our menu celebrates the everyday flavours people remember from Kerala bakeries, tea shops and Malabar kitchens.

We bring together Kerala special snacks, Malabar-style bakery favourites, Indian meals and tea in one comfortable place. Whether you visit for a quick snack or a relaxed meal, our goal is to make every visit feel welcoming.

What matters to us

Simple values. Honest food.

Tradition

We celebrate the food culture and flavours that make Kerala and Malabar special.

Freshness

We focus on fresh bakery items, snacks, tea and meals served throughout the day.

Hospitality

A friendly, comfortable dining experience for individuals, families and colleagues.

Our promise

Keep the flavour familiar.

From a simple cup of tea and snack to a satisfying Indian meal, we want our food to feel familiar, comforting and worth coming back for.
